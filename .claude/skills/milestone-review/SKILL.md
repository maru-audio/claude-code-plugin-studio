# Milestone Review

Milestone review: completeness, quality, risk and a go or no-go recommendation.

## Who leads

`producer`

## Steps

1. Read the milestone and its stories.
2. Read evidence, including listening reports.
3. List what is unverified.
4. Recommend go or no-go; the person decides.

## Done when

A recommendation exists with its evidence.
