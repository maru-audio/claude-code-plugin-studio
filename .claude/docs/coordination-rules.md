## Coordination rules

1. **Vertical delegation:** directors → leads → specialists. Do not skip tiers for a complex decision.
2. **Horizontal consultation:** same-tier agents may consult each other, but cannot make binding decisions outside their domain.
3. **Conflict resolution:** product and sound go to `creative-director`; technical goes to `technical-director`; scope and schedule go to `producer`.
4. **Change propagation:** when a change crosses departments, `producer` coordinates it. A contract change always touches presets, face and qualification in the same commit.
5. **Domain boundaries:** do not edit outside your domain without explicit delegation.
6. **Read by section:** read large design documents with offset and limit.
7. **Platform questions go to `maru-specialist`,** who answers from the installed CLI, never from memory.
